DPL-DOODLE-1.0 official illustration language. Attached project-owned approved drawings define ONLY shared broad visual language: rough FAT BLACK hand-marker outline with natural irregular thickness and fuzzy slightly uneven edge; extremely simple naive cute dot eyes and minimal black nose/mouth; large flat natural-color regions, a little uneven fill allowed, goofy easygoing slightly clumsy stance; somewhat enlarged head but retain BREED-SPECIFIC anatomy, ears, muzzle, body proportion, leg length and tail. Independent original dog, never reuse a reference body's silhouette. One complete dog only, square 1024x1024 transparent PNG RGBA, all ears paws and full natural tail entirely visible, subject centered with generous roughly 12% transparent margins. White fur must be opaque. No realistic fur strands, no many tiny tufts, no gradients, shading or highlights, no glass eyes, no 3D, no clean perfect vector curves, no logo or mascot treatment, no commercial polished sticker look. Tiny solid dot/oval eyes without highlights, no glossy nose. No text numbers watermark clothes accessories decorative elements ground cast shadow or background. At most 2-4 flat coat colors plus black lines. Use only very few broad wobbly contour bumps for coat. Do not imitate any third-party artist or copy their characters; only these original approved project dogs are the drawing-language references.
